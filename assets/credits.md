## Fonts

Knewave, Patrick Hand, and Nunito Sans come from the [official Google Fonts repository](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl). Each family retains its original SIL Open Font License in `fonts/*-license.txt`. Fonts are served locally.

## Decorative illustrations

`images/ornaments/sleepy-cats.webp` and `images/ornaments/studio-desk.webp` were generated with the built-in imagegen tool for this website. They are decoration, not official logos or game artwork. Alpha transparency is retained. Unused full-resolution PNGs were removed during repository cleanup. Small icons and sketch strokes are inline SVG in `index.html`.

## Generation prompts

### Sleeping cats

> Create one transparent PNG illustration asset for a warm cream-paper Indonesian indie game studio scrapbook website. Subject: three small sleepy cats cuddling together in a single low, wide horizontal group, left cat asleep with paws tucked, middle round cat resting chin on paw, right tabby looking gently at viewer with tiny curious eyes. Style: charming hand-drawn black/charcoal fine ink line art with slight imperfect pencil hatching, minimal warm beige patches, a restrained sketchbook doodle, relaxed and whimsical, not shiny, no 3D, no vector logo, no kawaii oversized eyes. Entire cats fully visible with generous transparent space around edges, no background, no paper rectangle, no text, no lettering, no watermark, no other objects. Composition 3 cats close together along the bottom in a wide group suited to a 300px-wide website margin ornament. This is decorative art, not an official logo or game artwork.

### Studio desk

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: transparent cutout editorial illustration for a personal indie game studio website. Draw a sleepy little tabby cat curled on top of an open sketchbook, a small classic game controller and one orange pencil resting beside the book. Three tiny hand-drawn ink sparkles above. Low horizontal composition, entire cat book controller and pencil fully inside generous transparent margins. Style: confident imperfect black ink and graphite pencil sketch, playful zine/scrapbook margin illustration, mostly monochrome charcoal fine lines with very sparse warm burnt-orange and cream pencil accents, no pastel watercolor, no glossy rendering, no vector geometry, not overly cute big eyes. Quiet relaxed mood, handmade and personal. Transparent background, no paper rectangle, no backdrop, no shadows outside objects, no text, no lettering, no logos, no watermark. This is a website ornament, not official game art or a studio logo.
